How Findora Works

Reconnecting with your lost belongings is straightforward with our 3-step community workflow.

01

1. Post a Report

Fill out a simple form detailing the item name, approximate location, date, and contact details. You can even upload a photo.

02

2. Search & Match

Use live keyword filters, categories, and status tags to easily check whether someone has already found and listed your item.

03

3. Reconnect & Claim

Reach out directly via provided email or phone. Verify ownership safely and pick up your belonging with peace of mind.

About The Platform

Building Trust, One Reunited Belonging at a Time

Findora was designed as a modern Lost & Found hub to solve the frustration of lost items on campuses, transit hubs, and public venues. Instead of scattered notice boards or lost social media posts, Findora centralizes inquiries into one easy-to-search repository.

  • Instant Frontend Synchronization: All posts are saved immediately in your browser's persistent storage.
  • Image Upload Preview: Add photo proof directly using file drag-and-drop or device camera.
  • Zero Clutter UI: Fast keyword matching, category tags, and responsive mobile-first architecture.

Project Information

Full Stack Development Demo

This web application was engineered strictly using semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 with gradient tokens and dark mode, and clean Vanilla JavaScript.

Data is managed in the client session using the Web Storage API (localStorage) so your posts persist across browser refreshes without requiring external database provisioning.

Community Safety Tips

Always arrange handover in well-lit, public locations like the student union or campus security desk. Request distinctive proof of ownership (e.g., unlocking password, wallpaper, serial number) before handing over valuables.
